The 2025_Edition branch is significantly ahead of main with a complete biome diversity system. Here's what it has:

Major additions:

Biome Diversity System - 8+ distinct biomes with unique characteristics
Biome Generator - Multi-layer noise-based terrain generation (elevation, moisture, temperature)
Biome Effect System - Gameplay modifiers affecting movement, energy, visibility, concealment
Habitat Component - Entities have biome preferences (preferred/avoided biomes)
Visual Enhancements - Biome-specific colors, patterns, animations, particle effects
